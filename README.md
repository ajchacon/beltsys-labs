## What is it?

Beltsys Labs is a development studio based in Tallinn (Estonia) with a team in Spain. We design and build blockchain and SaaS solutions for businesses, from initial consulting to production.

## Services

- 📜 **Smart contract development**: design, development and testing.
- 🏠 **Real-world asset (RWA) tokenization**: real estate, invoices and funds.
- 🔁 **dApps and DeFi**.
- 🖼️ **NFT marketplaces**.
- 🧭 **Blockchain consulting**: feasibility, architecture and network selection.
- ☁️ **Full-stack SaaS applications**.

## My role

As **Senior Project Development Manager** since 2016, I lead project analysis, architecture and delivery, and I also do hands-on full-stack and blockchain development.
